Welcome to the pilot of my college basketball blog! I am very excited to keep you guys updated with my rankings, predictions, and opinions throughout the season, as well as any news and notes from around the basketball world. The basketball season officially tips off in Rome with the battle of the Catholics, Notre Dame vs Villanova, on Sunday at 9:00 am. The offseason was as chaotic as ever this summer with the continued rise of transfers, coaching changes, and the formation of the newly reimagined Pac-12. This is my official season preview as we try to keep track of it all!

## My Final Four

```
Duke, Florida, Arizona, St. John's 
```

Duke may be the most talented team from top to bottom in a decade. This Duke squad is a great mix of returning contributors, impact transfers, and talented freshman. John Blackwell and Pat Ngongba headline the roster. However, it wouldn't shock anyone if neither one of them ends up as Duke's best player at the end of the year. The biggest challenge for Jon Scheyer might not be any one opponent but figuring out the best lineup to roll out on the court. 

Florida brings back nearly everyone once again. If the Gators see improved guard play alongside the dynamic trio of Thomas Haugh, Alex Condon, and Reuben Chinyelu, they are absolutely capable of cutting the nets down once again. 

Arizona is ranked No. 5 in the preseason AP Poll, and yet I still wonder if they are being underrated. Tommy Lloyd has made Arizona a perennial national title contender and I don't see that changing this year. Arizona loses a lot of key contributors from last year’s Final Four team. However, don’t expect them to take a step back. Caleb Holt is one of the best freshmen in the country and Ivan Karchenkov is primed for a breakout year. 

St. John's was a popular Final Four pick last year but never quite lived up the preseason expectations.  Well, I believe the hype was just a year early. St. John's starting 5 is possibly the best in the country led by Tounde Yessoufou and Quinn Ellis. Ellis's playmaking ability is a game changer and will lead to the best St. John's offense in the Rick Pitino era. 

## My National Champion

```
Duke
```

Duke has suffered back-to-back years of March heartbreaks. The weakness of those last two Duke teams has been late-game execution and self-inflicted mistakes in crucial moments. Jon Scheyer will have his best, most experienced ball handlers ever, alleviating some of those concerns. While this team may not have a Cameron Boozer or a Cooper Flagg, they instead have 11 starting-caliber players. Joaquim Boumtje Boumtje and Deron Rippey Jr will be two of the best freshman in the ACC and Drew Scharnowski is an excellent role player off the bench. If all the pieces mesh well for Duke, this could be one of the best Duke teams ever.
