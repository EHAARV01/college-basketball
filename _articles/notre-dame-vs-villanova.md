```
Notre Dame 74 - 72 Villanova
```

The college basketball season got off to a great start in Rome! Notre Dame battled Villanova in the first-ever Eternal City Tip-Off in the lone game of the day. I loved having an international game to start off the season, and I hope to see this continued in the future. The game itself was a thriller: Notre Dame squeaked out a come from behind victory 74-72. 

### Villanova Reactions

Villanova had high expectations coming into Kevin Willard's second season; however, opening night did not go as planned. The Wildcats got off to a rocky start, dropping their first game of the year. The story of the night was the abundance of turnovers, a problem that reared its head in their exhibition loss against Virginia. Notre Dame forced a concerning 20 more today, 15 of them coming in the first half. Villanova at times gave up open jumpers off pick-and-roll actions when Luigi Suigo was in the game, and Devin Royal shockingly did not make a field goal. On the bright side, Tyler Perkins looks like he has taken another step forward from last year, and Kwame Evans Jr. flashed his immense potential on the defensive end. The Wildcats will improve as they find their identity and get more comfortable playing in a system together. Villanova hosts Holy Cross on Friday, looking to bounce back in its home opener.

### Notre Dame Reactions

This was a welcome surprise for many Fighting Irish fans. Despite being picked last in the preseason ACC poll, Notre Dame starts off the season on a positive note. Notre Dame was powered by Braeden Smith, Braeden Shrewsberry, and Brady Koehler, who combined for 47 of the Irish's 74 points. Koehler was extremely impressive in his sophomore debut, putting up 16 points, 8 rebounds, 2 blocks, and 2 steals. Notre Dame also got valuable minutes from Ethan Roberts, a transfer from Pennsylvania, and Logan Duncomb, who hit a huge three to take the lead with a minute left. This Notre Dame team might not be very deep; however, they are going to be a feisty team in the ACC and may surprise some people this year.
